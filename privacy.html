Privacy

Last updated 7 September 2026

BadgeBudget is a take-home pay planner for nurses. It was built for one nurse and her unit, and it is run by one person, not a company.

Your pay figures are never sent to analytics, never sold, and never shared with anyone. Paystub PDFs are read on your phone and never uploaded. If you don't sign in, nothing you enter leaves your device at all.

What is stored, and where

If you are not signed in

Everything — your rate, differentials, shifts, notes, goals and patterns — is saved in your browser's local storage on that device only. It is never sent to a server. Clearing your browser data deletes it, and there is no copy anywhere else.

If you sign in

The same information is saved to a database so it can sync between your phone and other devices. It is stored as a single record attached to your account and is readable only by you — the database enforces this per-row, not just in the app.

Your account

Sign-in uses Google. BadgeBudget receives your email address and name from Google and stores no password. Nothing is posted to your Google account and no other Google data is requested.

What is never collected

  • Pay figures. Usage analytics record that a shift was saved, never what it was worth. Wage and savings-goal amounts are excluded by design.
  • Paystub contents. A paystub PDF is parsed entirely on your device to read your rate and differentials. The file is never uploaded and nothing from it is transmitted.
  • Shift titles from your calendar. Calendar import reads dates, times, hours and event identifiers only — never the text of an event.

Usage analytics

To know which parts of the app get used, BadgeBudget records coarse events — for example shift_saved, setup_completed, swap_posted. Each carries the page path, your browser's user-agent string, and a random device identifier generated in your browser. That identifier is not linked to your name and is not shared with anyone.

There are no advertising trackers, no analytics SDKs, no cookies for tracking, and no data brokers. Nothing is sold or licensed.

Error reports

When something goes wrong, the app keeps the error message, the file it came from, and the line number, and sends that batch the next time you open the app — so that failures on real phones are visible enough to fix. Amounts that look like money are stripped out before sending, and error traces are not transmitted.

Feedback

If you use the feedback button, your message is stored along with the page you were on and your browser's user-agent string. An email address is optional — supply one only if you want a reply.

Calendar sync

If you connect a calendar, its secret address is stored in a separate table readable only by you. It is deliberately kept out of the data record that gets exported, mirrored to your device and synced between devices, and it is never written to logs.

The shift-swap board

Swap posts are anonymous to the rest of your unit. Names are revealed only after everyone involved in a swap has accepted it. Anonymity is enforced by the database rather than by the app.

One honest limitation, also stated inside the app: each poster has a stable anonymous handle within a group. If a colleague learns who you are through one completed swap, they can recognise your later posts in that same group.

Who else can see any of this

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages serves the site, and as with any website its servers see your IP address when the page loads.
  • Google is involved only if you use Google sign-in.
  • The app loads its fonts and code libraries from Google Fonts, unpkg, cdnjs and jsDelivr. Like any site that loads external files, those services see your IP address. They receive nothing about you or your pay.

No one else. There are no third-party integrations beyond these.

Your data, your call

  • Export it. Settings → Export gives you everything as a JSON file.
  • Delete it. Settings → Clear all data erases the copy on your device and, if you are signed in, deletes your record from the database and signs you out. This cannot be undone.

Clearing your data does not automatically remove past analytics events, submitted feedback, or the sign-in account itself. Email the address below and those will be deleted too.

Children

BadgeBudget is intended for working nurses and is not directed at children.
