Terms of use

Last updated 6 November 2026

BadgeBudget gives you an estimate, not tax, payroll or legal advice. Every dollar figure in the app — take-home pay, a differential, overtime, a savings goal — is a projection built from the numbers you entered.

Your employer's paystub is the record. Where the app and your paystub disagree, the paystub is right and the app is wrong. Don't use a figure from BadgeBudget to settle a question with payroll, your union, a lender or the IRS.

Who runs this

BadgeBudget is built and run by one person, not a company. It started as a planner for one nurse and her unit. There is no support team and no service agreement behind it; there is an email address at the bottom of this page, read by the person who writes the code.

What it costs

Nothing. There is no subscription, no trial, no payment of any kind, and nothing in the app sells your attention or your data to anyone. Because you are not paying for a service, nothing here is a promise that the app will keep working, keep its current features, or stay online.

The estimate, in detail

This is the part worth reading twice, because it is the whole product.

  • The app computes, it does not know. It uses the base rate, differentials, shift lengths, pre-tax deductions and filing assumptions you gave it. Wrong input, wrong answer, with no way for the app to tell.
  • Taxes are approximated. Federal, state and local withholding, FICA and pre-tax buckets are modelled with simplified rules. Real withholding depends on your W-4, your employer's payroll system, year-to-date totals and local rules the app does not see.
  • Premiums and overtime follow your contract, not ours. Differentials, overtime multipliers, work-period rules, court or call-back minimums and lump-sum payments are whatever you configured. Your contract or employer policy governs what you are actually owed.
  • A projection is not a promise of pay. Picking up a shift because the app said it was worth a certain amount is your decision, made on an estimate.
  • Calendar sync can be wrong or out of date. Imported shifts come from a third-party calendar feed. Dates, times and lengths are read as published; a change your employer makes after the feed is read is not in the app until it syncs again.
  • Paystub reading is a convenience. When the app reads a rate out of a paystub PDF it is pattern-matching on your device. Check what it filled in.

Your account and your data

You can use BadgeBudget without signing in at all, in which case your data stays in your browser on that device. If you do sign in, your data is stored so it can sync between your devices. What is stored, what is never collected, and how to export or delete it is set out in the privacy notice, which is part of these terms.

Keep your sign-in to yourself — anyone who can get into your account can read your pay history. An account is for one person; don't share one. Tell us if you think someone else has access to it.

You can take your data out at any time (Settings → Export) and delete it at any time (Settings → Clear all data). Deleting is not reversible, and there is no backup to restore it from.

What you put into it

Your shifts, notes, goals and anything else you type stay yours. By entering them you give BadgeBudget only the permission it needs to run: to store them, sync them to your own devices, and show them back to you. Nothing you enter is sold, licensed or shared for advertising.

If you use the shift-swap board, a post you make is shown to other signed-in members of that board, under a per-group handle rather than your name. Read the limits of that handle in the privacy notice before posting; treat a post as visible to your unit. Don't post anyone else's personal information, a patient's details, or anything you are not free to share.
